No question is too small.❓

Here are a few things children, parents, and teachers ask before their first PLO adventure.

How does a student register for the competition? ▼
  • Individual Registration ↗
  • Group Registration ↗
What knowledge and skills do I need? ▼
  • In short: You definitely need a fresh brain, couple of pencils and pens and a lot of patience!

  • Long version: You mostly need logical thinking, as well as general awareness of directions, calendars, etc. You do not need prior knowledge of linguistics, computer science, programming, or foreign languages.
How long is the Round 1 contest?▼
The contest will last for 4 hours.
What is the registration fee? ▼
  • Single Registration for Track1 (Regular) costs ₹1000 + ₹180(GST), totaling ₹1180
  • Single Registration for Track2 (Access) costs ₹300 + ₹54(GST), totaling ₹354
  • Group Registration ↗
What happens if I register after 10 November 2026? ▼
Registration is open until 20 November 2026. However, participants whose registrations are received after 10 November will be accommodated through the walk-in arrangement. Examination centres will be confirmed only for registrations received on or before 10 November 2026.
Can the same email ID be used for multiple registrations? ▼

No. Each registration must be made using a unique email ID. The same email ID cannot be used for multiple registrations.

Can students who have completed Class 12 or are repeating Class 12 participate?▼
Contestants must:
  1. Be under 20 years of age on the opening day of the IOL; and
  2. Be currently attending secondary school (high school) or a lower grade, or have only recently completed secondary school.
Can I get a refund if I can’t attend after registering? ▼
Unfortunately, no. Registration fees are non-refundable.
How many problems should I expect? ▼
Expect around 6–8 problems in the contest booklet.
Where can I find example problems and related reading materials?▼
  • You can find some reading materials under the Resources tab.
  • Note that these are not required reading to participate.
  • You can also find past problems under the Resources tab.
  • You can find even more problems by going to the other Linguistics Olympiads section below or by surfing the web. 😉
