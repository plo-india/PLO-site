📋 Instructions to the Candidate

Mandatory Compliance
⏰

Reporting & Identity Verification

  • Report 90 minutes before exam start.
  • Bring Admit Card + Original Photo ID (School ID, Aadhaar Card, Passport, etc.). No photocopies.
  • Leaving before 1:00 PM is prohibited.
🚫

Prohibited Items

  • Banned: Papers, books, digital watches, phones.
  • Only simple analog watches allowed (no memory/connectivity).
  • Possession = immediate debarment.
📝

Stationery & Rough Work

  • Rough sheets provided in exam hall.
  • Write Name & Roll Number on sheets.
  • Return all rough sheets before exit.
🎒

Allowed Personal Items

  • Bring your own pen and pencil.
  • Recommended: Water bottle & snacks.
  • Pocket hand sanitizer allowed.
