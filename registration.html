TRACK 2

Access Track

Applicable to students who meet any one of the following criteria:

A. Regional Language Medium Students
Students whose medium of school education is an Indian regional language, where non-language subjects are taught in that regional language.

B. English Medium Students from Lower-Income Families
Students whose medium of school education is English and whose annual family income is less than INR 6 lakh.

C. Students of Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalayas (JNVs)
Students currently enrolled in a JNV.

INR 354

INR 300 + 18% GST


Register for Access Track →
TRACK 3

Group registration

Schools may register students as a group for the PLO examination. Group registrations are eligible for discounted registration fees as per the details below:

Group Registration Guidelines
  • A minimum of 10 participants is required to qualify for group registration.
  • Group discounts are applied only to the base registration fee (GST will be charged additionally as applicable).
  • The applicable discounts are as follows:
    • 10–20 participants: 10% discount
    • 21–30 participants: 12% discount
    • 31–50 participants: 15% discount
    • More than 50 participants: 20% discount
  • Group registrations must be completed through a single consolidated payment and participant list.
  • The registration fee is non-refundable.

Registration and Payment →
Mandatory Documents

All tracks require valid Student ID cards.


Track 2/Track3 requires below mentioned documents:

  1. For Regional Language Medium Students:
    • Official School Bonafide Certificate verifying the regional-language medium of instruction
  2. For English Medium Students with annual family income below INR 6 lakh:
    • Valid income certificate/documentary proof establishing annual family income of less than INR 6 lakh
  3. For JNV Students:
    • School Bonafide Certificate confirming current enrollment, if required

Maximum File Size: Each uploaded document must not exceed 2 MB.

Selection Criteria

The national team selection follows the International Linguistics Olympiad (IOL) transparency and accreditation regulations.

1

Round 1 — 29 November 2026

Round 1 is open to all registered candidates. The top qualifying students from Round 1 will be invited to participate in Round 2.

2

Round 2 and Team Selection

Top performers from Round 2 will be evaluated, and the top 4 students will be selected to represent the country as Team India 1 at the IOL.

A second team may participate at their own expense if the local arrangements allow it, at the discretion of the Local Organising Committee..

Transparency Regarding Fees and Travel Costs

National Round Fees

The registration fee charged covers the basic administrative and operational expenses of conducting the national selection rounds.

IOL Participation and Travel Costs

Depending on available sponsorship and organizational funding, efforts will be made to cover or subsidize participation costs for Team India 1. Please note that sponsorship or funding cannot be guaranteed.

If Team India 2 is selected, all expenditures must be borne directly by the individual participants, including:

  • Official IOL registration fees
  • Visa processing fees
  • International travel and flight tickets
  • Other associated travel and participation expenses
