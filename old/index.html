Welcome to The Panini Linguistics Olympiad!

For curious minds studying 6–12

Solve the language Puzzles

PLO is a fun, exciting, educational, challenging, puzzling, linguistic competition for students studying in grades 6th to 8th (Juniors) and 9th to 12th (Seniors). This competition helps foster problem solving, computational thinking and appreciation for the linguistic diversity and linguistics in general.
